Our story

We believe a good question can start a better conversation than a lecture.

The Quiz Initiative was started by Aniruddha Rudra to make knowledge accessible to every student. We run free quiz competitions and learning sessions on campuses, so curiosity, critical thinking and teamwork become part of everyday student life.

What we bring to your campus

Every session is free for the institution and open to students. Formats can be adapted to your audience, subject areas and available time.

Quiz competitions

Team-based rounds on general knowledge, current affairs, science, history, culture and more, run live with a host and a scoreboard.

Knowledge challenges

Short, themed challenges that reward reasoning over recall: puzzles, connect-the-clues rounds and rapid-fire questions.

Interactive learning sessions

Hands-on sessions where students discuss answers, question assumptions and learn how to find reliable information.

How a campus quiz comes together

Four steps from first message to final scoreboard. We handle the questions, hosting and format.

  1. Send a request

    Tell us about your institution, audience and preferred dates using the form below.

  2. Plan together

    We agree on format, topics, team sizes, timing and the space you can offer.

  3. Host the event

    Our team runs the quiz on campus. Students take part for free.

  4. Keep it going

    We share a short wrap-up, and your students can help organise the next round.

What we stand for

These principles shape every question we write and every event we run.

Access first

Knowledge should not depend on fees or location. Our events are free for institutions and students.

Curiosity over cramming

We reward the habit of asking why, not only the ability to remember.

Critical thinking

Questions are built to make people weigh evidence, spot patterns and explain their reasoning.

Community

Quizzes work best in teams. We design for mixed groups where everyone gets a turn.
